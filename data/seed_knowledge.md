## Company overview
Cyberathon Technology Pvt. Ltd. (also written CyberAthon Technology (OPC) Pvt. Ltd.) is an IT and software company based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. It builds software for startups, SMEs and enterprises. The website mentions 10+ years of experience. Tagline: "Leading IT Company for Digital Transformation" and "Innovating with a digital mindset".

## What the company does - focus areas
Cyberathon focuses on data analytics, big data, process automation, and desktop and web solutions. It delivers custom software, websites, mobile apps, digital marketing and IT consulting.
